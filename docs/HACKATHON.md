Winning team goes to ESPC 2026 in Amsterdam: 4-day conference tickets, hotel, flights,
fully covered.

## What the organisers asked for

Directly from the post:

- *"Bring a workflow that is slower than it should be, a customer question you keep
  hearing, or just curiosity."* You don't need a finished idea to join.
- *"Local AI opens up more than just where processing happens. It brings new
  possibilities around performance, responsiveness, privacy, security and cost control."*
- *"Your ideas won't stop with the hackathon — they could become example cases for our
  customers, real solutions for Atea."*

That last line is the one that should shape what we build. They are looking for
something repeatable, not a one-off toy.

## Who's judging

At least one organiser is a **Director Cloud & DevOps**, alongside a **strategic
advisor**. That mix matters:

- The technical half will smell hand-waving immediately, and will ask the hard question
  about whatever we claim. Have the honest answer ready rather than the confident one.
- The strategic half is looking for something Atea could offer customers, not just use
  internally.
- Neither of them works in finance or accounts payable. That shaped our choice — see
  `DECISIONS.md`.
