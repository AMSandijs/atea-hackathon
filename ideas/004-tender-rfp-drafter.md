# 004 — Tender / RFP drafter

**One sentence:** local vault of past proposals plus a new tender document, drafting
answers with citations back to the source.

**Status: PARKED.**

---

## The four questions

**1. Is the AI load-bearing?** Entirely. This is retrieval and drafting; there's no
non-AI version.

**2. Why must it run locally?** Airtight. Public-sector tenders are confidential by
default and often carry explicit no-third-party-processing clauses.

**3. What's the 90-second demo?** Weak. Text in, text out. Judging draft quality live is
slow and subjective.

**4. Who has this problem, and what's the number?** Atea bids tenders constantly, and the
value per win is enormous. But we have no named person and no measured time cost.

## Known weaknesses

Highest business value per unit of effort of anything we listed, and the worst demo. Also
needs real past proposals to be any good, which is a permission conversation we don't
have time for.

## Verdict

**Park.** Genuinely worth proposing to the business separately from the hackathon.
