## The four questions

Answer these honestly; a weak answer here is worth more than a strong-sounding one.
See `docs/WORKING-WITH-AI.md` for what each one means.

**1. Is the AI load-bearing?**
> Remove the model. What breaks?

**2. Why must it run locally?**
> What specifically cannot leave the machine, and who says so?

**3. What's the 90-second demo?**
> Beat by beat. What does the room actually see happen?

**4. Who has this problem, and what's the number?**
> A name and a frequency. "Estimated" is fine if marked as such.
